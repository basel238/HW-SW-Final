05. Challenge how bottlenecks and limits were inferred
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User prompt:
"Can you help me firts better undertsand from the results how do we conclude
 the bottlenecks you talked about?
 how did you infer these limits on the benchmarks from seeing this data and
 flamegraphs?"

Decision/outcome: connect hot source operations to Python calls and native
symbols, while correcting overconfident interpretations. A debug interpreter
self-sample share is not a precise removable fraction of release runtime;
whole-program instructions divided by calls do not measure a fixed call cost;
static bytecode counts are not percentages of executed time. Amdahl estimates
must use a defensible scope and must include the replacement's cost.

Evidence: report_raytrace.txt; report_nbody.txt; selected folded stacks,
perf dumps, counters and cProfile outputs; preserved correction history.
